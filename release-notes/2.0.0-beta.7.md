Seventh beta of **Crack Attack! — Back From The Stack**.

- **Terminal version, a real game**: it now opens on a menu (Solo, Vs computer, Vs player), with
  a countdown, the round number and result in big block letters, and a pause. A duel against the
  computer or another player shows the opponent's board beside yours, and a round's result stays
  on screen until you press Enter.
- **Terminal version, against other players**: the *Vs player* page is a waiting room: being on it
  makes you joinable, and you see the players who wait too. On the local network the terminal
  finds games by itself (as the graphical game does) and **plays against the graphical game**, in
  both directions. For a game served over ssh, `--lobby DIR` makes the players of one server see
  each other, and `--invite-command TEXT` shows how to invite a friend.
- **Terminal version, command line**: the options follow the graphical game's: `--vs
  network-create [PORT]`, `--vs network-join HOST [PORT]`, `-n/--name`, `--difficulty`,
  `-f/--max-frames`, `-s/--seed`, `--autoplay` with `--autoplay-ai`, `--preset`, and the matching
  `--open` and `--set`. `--no-lan` turns the search for local players off. **Changed**: the level
  is no longer written after `--vs` or `--autoplay` (use `--difficulty` and `--autoplay-ai`),
  `--frames` is now `--max-frames`, and `--replay` is gone.

Network play stays compatible with beta 6 (and beta 5, 4 and 3, but not beta 2). Packages installed
from beta 6 keep their settings, scores and ranking.

This is a beta: please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/thiosaur/crackattack/issues).
