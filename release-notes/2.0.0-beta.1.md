First public beta of **Crack Attack! — Back From The Stack**, a Rust rewrite of the
classic Crack Attack! (Daniel R. Nelson, 2000–2006) with a WebGPU renderer and SDL3.
The gameplay follows the original 1.1.14; everything around it is new.

- **Modes**: solo, versus the computer (three difficulty levels), and versus another player
  on your local network, found automatically or by address, with a network ranking.
- **New look**: redrawn logo, countdown and messages, new garbage textures, a window that
  adapts to any size.
- **New sound**: all-new sound effects, and music based on the song "Les Crapauds"
  (1897). During a game the music follows the pressure on your stack: it grows more
  intense as the stack climbs and settles when you recover.
- **Controls**: keyboard and gamepad everywhere, fully navigable menus, rebindable keys.
- **Languages**: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Russian, Turkish, Simplified
  Chinese, Japanese and Korean. All but English and French are new: please report anything that
  reads wrong.
- **Updates**: a daily check tells you when a newer version is published (it can be turned
  off in *About → Updates*).

This is a beta: please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/thiosaur/crackattack/issues).
