Fourth beta of **Crack Attack! — Back From The Stack**.

- **Terminal version**: the text version (`crackattack-terminal`, built from source) is
  redone: bracket cursor, half-row steps, the game logo and key hints in a side panel,
  a *Game over* animation, and lines cut to the window width. It can also be played by the
  computer (`--autoplay`) and write text captures of the screen.
- **In game**: the logo is a little higher in the left column.
- **Credits**: the origin of the computer opponent's strategy is now credited in `AUTHORS`
  and in the README.

Network play stays compatible with beta 3 (but not with beta 2). Packages installed from
beta 3 keep their settings, scores and ranking.

This is a beta: please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/thiosaur/crackattack/issues).
