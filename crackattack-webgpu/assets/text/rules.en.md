# How to play

Goal: in VS, bury your opponent under garbage; in Solo, get the highest score.

- Move the cursor with the arrow keys.
- Move the cursor to select **two cells side by side**, then swap their blocks (Space by default). You can also swap a block with an empty cell.
- Line up **3 or more blocks** of the same color, horizontally or vertically, to clear them.
- Cascading clears make **chains**; several lines at once make **combos**. Chains and combos send garbage to your opponent.
- Hold Enter to raise the stack faster.
- If the stack reaches the top, you have **seven seconds** to lower it before you lose.
